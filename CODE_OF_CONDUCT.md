# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.
Be considerate, focus criticism on the work, document scientific assumptions,
and welcome questions from contributors with different levels of experience.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
personal attacks, or publishing another person's private information without
permission. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be handled
as confidentially and promptly as practical.
